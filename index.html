El7a2ny

Automotive services platform

Four connected apps

El7a2ny connects car owners with service providers for booking and scheduling car services. The platform is four apps sharing one backend: customers, service providers, workers and administrators.

  • Booking and scheduling flows across all four roles, with real-time updates on each request.
  • Online payments through Paymob.
  • API documented with Swagger; deployed on Railway with a custom domain.

  • NestJS
  • PostgreSQL
  • Next.js
  • Paymob
